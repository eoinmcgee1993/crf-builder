8. ECU templates — read this part properly

An ECU template is a starting point, not a finished tune. It is a set of values for a naturally-aspirated engine on pump fuel, written for a bike with a free-flowing aftermarket exhaust and intake. It has not been tuned to your particular motorcycle, your altitude, your fuel, or your state of wear.

Flashing a map is capable of destroying an engine. Running a lean mixture, an aggressive ignition curve, or the decel-flame settings on a catalysed factory exhaust can hole a piston, damage valves or destroy the catalytic converter. Verify air/fuel ratio on a dyno or with a wideband sensor before extended use.

You use these files at your own risk, on your own machine, on your own judgement. We are not liable for engine, exhaust or vehicle damage, for injury, or for any consequence of flashing, riding on, or modifying a motorcycle using them.

Knowing and following your local law is your responsibility, not ours. Modification will usually void your manufacturer's warranty.
