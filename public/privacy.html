Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number, CVC or expiry — those go directly to Stripe. We receive only a payment reference, the amount, and whether it succeeded. Stripe's own privacy policy governs what they hold: stripe.com/privacy.

2. Children's data

Youth garment sizes are offered, so a club roster may contain the names, race numbers and sizes of children. We ask that rosters are submitted only by a parent, guardian, or an authorised club official who has the parents' permission for those details to be used to make the kit.

Children's names and numbers are used for one purpose only: printing them on the garments ordered. They are never used for marketing, never shared beyond the manufacturer producing that order, and are deleted on request without question.

3. What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and there is no analytics or advertising pixel on these tools.
  • We do not use your uploaded artwork for anything except producing your order. It is not published, resold, or used as a sample without your explicit permission.
  • We do not send marketing to rider names collected on a roster.

4. Who else can see it

Your data is processed by a small number of providers, each only for the function described:

  • Supabase — the database holding orders and artwork.
  • Netlify — hosts the site and receives ECU Template Builder email submissions.
  • Stripe — processes payments.
  • The manufacturer or print shop producing your order, who receives only what is needed to make it.

These providers operate internationally, so your data may be processed outside your own country.

5. How long we keep it

  • Orders and roster details: kept while the order is produced and for a reasonable period after, so we can handle reprints, disputes and accounting obligations.
  • Uploaded artwork and finished files: kept so you can re-download a file you paid for, and so a kit can be reproduced if damaged in transit.
  • Email addresses from the ECU tool: kept until you ask to be removed.

7. Security

Orders are held in a database where the website's public key can create an order but cannot read any order back — so no visitor can browse other people's orders through the site. Payment status can only be set by a cryptographically verified notification from Stripe, never by a browser. Files you have paid for are released only against a private token issued with your order.

No system is perfect, and we do not claim otherwise. If you believe you have found a problem, please tell us at [EMAIL NOT SET].
